## Standards the code follows

- ITU-R BS.1770-4 (K-weighted loudness, gating) and EBU R128 / Tech 3342 (loudness range). The meter is an independent implementation, checked against ffmpeg's `ebur128` filter on the example (-15.0 against -15.1 LUFS).
- Equal-power panning and standard biquad filter forms (Audio EQ Cookbook by Robert Bristow-Johnson); reverb impulse responses are synthetic decaying noise with early reflections.

## Influences on the method

- **sourceframe** (same author): the idea that the picture's code is the source of truth; cuelab reads the same `timeline.json`.
- **creator-skills by endlessconflict** ([github.com/endlessconflict/creator-skills](https://github.com/endlessconflict/creator-skills)): the direct, build, critique loop with a kill-list and a stop rule.
- The craft of interface sound (short pitched sounds, one timbre family, ducking under focal events) is common knowledge; no library or sound pack was used.

## The first piece this was distilled from

A 77-second explainer on how coreboot runs C before DRAM exists, scored with 619 cues. The coreboot project (GPL-2.0, https://www.coreboot.org) was its subject; that video and its sound are not part of this repository.
